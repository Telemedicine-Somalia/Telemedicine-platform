## 📋 Brief Description

Telemedicine Somalia is a comprehensive telemedicine platform that connects patients with healthcare providers, enabling remote healthcare delivery and convenient medical consultations. This research-based system addresses key challenges in modern healthcare delivery by integrating appointment management, real-time notifications, automated reminders, prescription management, and laboratory request workflows into a unified platform.

The system supports both web-based administrative interfaces and mobile application integration through RESTful APIs, enabling seamless communication between patients, healthcare providers, and administrative staff.

## How to Use Key Features

### Booking an Appointment (Patients)

1. **Navigate to Video Consultations**
   - From the home screen, tap on the "Consultation" icon

2. **Browse Doctors**
   - Browse available doctors by specialty
   - Tap on a doctor card to view their profile

3. **Select Doctor**
   - Review doctor information:
     - Qualifications and experience
     - Specialty areas
     - Ratings and reviews
     - Available time slots

4. **Choose Appointment Time**
   - Select a date from the calendar
   - Choose an available time slot
   - Review appointment details

5. **Confirm Booking**
   - Review appointment summary
   - Make payment if required
   - Confirm the booking
   - You'll receive a confirmation notification

6. **Attend Appointment**
   - You'll receive a reminder before the appointment
   - Join the video call at the scheduled time
   - Consult with your doctor
   - Complete the consultation

### Conducting a Video Consultation (Doctors)

1. **Access Appointments**
   - Open the "Appointments" section from your dashboard
   - View scheduled appointments for the day

2. **Start Consultation**
   - Select an upcoming appointment
   - Review patient information and history
   - Tap "Start Consultation" or "Join Call"

3. **During Consultation**
   - Conduct the video consultation
   - Review patient symptoms and concerns
   - Access patient medical history if needed
   - Take consultation notes

4. **After Consultation**
   - Complete consultation notes
   - Create prescriptions if needed
   - Request laboratory tests if required
   - End the consultation

### Viewing Prescriptions (Patients)

1. **Access My Treatment**
   - Navigate to "My Treatment" from the home screen
   - Or access from appointment details

2. **View Prescriptions**
   - Select a completed consultation
   - View associated prescription
   - Review medication details and instructions

3. **Download Prescription**
   - Tap on the prescription to view full details
   - Use the download or print option if available
   - Save for your records

### Creating a Prescription (Doctors)

1. **After Consultation**
   - Complete the consultation with patient
   - Navigate to prescription creation

2. **Add Medications**
   - Enter medication name
   - Specify dosage and frequency
   - Add instructions for use
   - Set duration of treatment

3. **Save Prescription**
   - Review prescription details
   - Save and send to patient
   - Patient will receive notification

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### General Questions

**Q: Is the app free to download?**
A: Yes, the Telemedicine Somalia app is free to download. Charges apply only for consultations and services.

**Q: Can I use the app without internet?**
A: Some features require internet connectivity, especially for video consultations and real-time updates. However, you can view previously downloaded content offline.

**Q: Is my medical information secure?**
A: Yes, we use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect all your medical and personal information.

**Q: Can I cancel an appointment?**
A: Yes, you can cancel appointments through the app. Please check the cancellation policy which may vary based on timing.

**Q: How do I update my profile?**
A: Navigate to your profile from the home screen, tap on the profile picture or menu, and select "Edit Profile" to update your information.

### Appointment Questions

**Q: How far in advance can I book an appointment?**
A: Appointment booking availability depends on each doctor's schedule. Typically, you can book appointments up to several weeks in advance.

**Q: Can I reschedule an appointment?**
A: Yes, you can reschedule appointments through the app, subject to doctor availability.

**Q: What happens if I miss my appointment?**
A: Contact support or the doctor directly. Policies may vary regarding missed appointments.

### Payment Questions

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: The app accepts various payment methods including credit cards, debit cards, and mobile payments. Specific methods depend on your region.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**
A: Refund policies vary. Please contact customer support for refund inquiries.

**Q: Are payments secure?**
A: Yes, all payments are processed through secure, encrypted payment gateways.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Which devices are supported?**
A: The app supports Android (version 8.0 and above) and iOS (version 12.0 and above) devices.

**Q: How do I update the app?**
A: Download the latest version from the [Releases](https://github.com/Telemedicine-Somalia/Telemedicine-platform/releases) section and install it.

**Q: What should I do if the app crashes?**
A: Try restarting the app, clearing cache, or reinstalling. If issues persist, contact technical support.
